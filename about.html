Who we are

MADAM is a student project about gender equality. We built it to learn, to share what we found, and to give people a place to speak without having to give their name.

The name

It reads the same both ways

Forward or backward, MADAM doesn't change. We liked that. It's how we think people should be treated, whichever side they're standing on.

The name M.A.D.A.M. (Making A Difference, Always Magnificent) represents a group that believes in the power of unity, equality, and friendship. The logo combines the female and male symbols into an infinity sign, representing that both genders are equally important and should always be treated with respect. It symbolizes the coming together of different identities, showing that differences should never be a reason for division. As M.A.D.A.M., we aim to make a difference through our teamwork, support, and shared belief that everyone deserves equal opportunities.

Who it's for

Everyone, honestly

This isn't women against men. Rigid gender roles hurt a lot of people: women who get paid less, men who are told not to cry, LGBTQ+ people who are told they don't belong.

If you think fairness shouldn't depend on gender, this is for you.
